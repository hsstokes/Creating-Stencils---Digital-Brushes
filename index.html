Task 1
✂️ Apply a Stencil to Oil Pastel Texture

Watch the video first.
Then follow the steps. Click a step to open it.

▶ Watch: Creating and applying stencils

Open your texture (oil pastel scan) and your stencil image in Photoshop.

Go to Image › Adjustments › Threshold.

Your image is now black and white.

  1. Use the Rectangular Marquee Tool M. Select a small white area.
  2. Go to Select › Similar. Now all the white areas are selected.
  3. Press Delete.

Use the Move Tool V.
Place your stencil on the texture.

  1. Create a selection.
  2. Press Delete.
  3. Deselect: Cmd + D
  1. Save as FULL_NAME_stencil.jpg
  2. Take a screenshot of your process for Task 3.

🆘 Stuck? Try this
I press Delete and nothing happens.
Look at the Layers panel (Window › Layers). Click the correct layer first. Then press Delete.
A “Fill” window opens when I press Delete.
You are on a locked Background layer. Click Cancel. Double-click the Background layer, then click OK. Try again.
I deleted the wrong part.
Undo: Cmd + Z
The marching ants won’t go away.
Deselect: Cmd + D
My stencil is too big or too small.
Free Transform: Cmd + T. Drag a corner. Press Enter.

Task 2
🖌️ Create a Digital Brush from Your Stencil

Watch the video first.
Then follow the steps. Click a step to open it.

▶ Watch: Creating custom digital brushes

Select the stencil shape you want to use.

Go to Edit › Define Brush Preset.

Select the Brush Tool.
Choose a colour.

Create a new layer.
Apply your brush.

Try different colours.
Try different blend modes.

  1. Use your brush in at least 3 different ways.
  2. Save as FULL_NAME_digital_brush.jpg
  3. Take a screenshot of your process for Task 3.

Creative ideas

Research pages · click to enlarge
🆘 Stuck? Try this
My brush is a big rectangle.
Select only your stencil shape before Edit › Define Brush Preset.
My brush is grey or faint.
Black = strong paint. Grey = weak paint. Use Threshold to make your stencil pure black first.
My brush covers the whole page.
Make it smaller: press [ many times, or change Size at the top of the screen.
I can’t find my brush.
Open Window › Brushes. Your brush is at the bottom of the list.
Nothing paints.
Check you are on a normal layer, not a hidden or locked one. Check your colour is not white.
